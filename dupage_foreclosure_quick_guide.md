# DuPage County Foreclosure Sales - Quick Reference Guide

## 🎯 Fastest Way to Find Foreclosure Sales

### Option 1: Call Sheriff's Office (RECOMMENDED)
**Phone**: (630) 682-7256  
**Alternative**: (630) 407-2000 (ask for Civil Division)  
**Ask for**: Current foreclosure sale listings

### Option 2: Check ILFLS (Illinois Foreclosure Listing Service) ⭐ **RECOMMENDED**
**ILFLS**: https://ilfls.com/free-daily-auction-lists/dupage  
- **FREE daily foreclosure auction lists for DuPage County**
- Case numbers, addresses, estimated values
- Mortgage balances and auction details
- Updated daily

### Option 3: Check Other Online Platforms
**Auction.com**: https://www.auction.com (search DuPage County)  
**Redfin**: https://www.redfin.com/county/733/IL/DuPage-County/foreclosures  
**Zillow**: https://www.zillow.com/dupage-county-il/foreclosures/

### Option 4: Attend Sales in Person
**Location**: 501 N. County Farm Road, Wheaton, IL 60187  
**Schedule**: **Tuesdays and Thursdays at 10:00 AM**  
**Note**: Sales are public auctions - arrive early to review properties
