## Understanding Foreclosure Sales in DuPage County

### Process:
1. **Foreclosure Case Filed**: A lender files a foreclosure case in DuPage County Circuit Court (Chancery Division)
2. **Court Judgment**: Court enters judgment of foreclosure
3. **Sale Scheduled**: Approximately 3 months after judgment, sale is scheduled
4. **Sale Conducted**: Sheriff's Office conducts public auction
5. **Redemption Period**: Property owner may have redemption rights after sale

### Key Information:
- Foreclosure sales are **public auctions**
- Sales are conducted by the **Sheriff's Office**
- Sale dates are set by **court order**
- Notices are **published in local newspapers** (required by law)
- Properties are sold **as-is**

## Important Notes

⚠️ **Foreclosure Sales vs. Tax Sales**
- **Foreclosure Sales**: Properties being foreclosed by lenders (mortgage defaults)
- **Tax Sales**: Properties with delinquent property taxes (different process)
- Make sure you're looking at the right type of sale!

⚠️ **Due Diligence Required**
- Properties are sold **as-is**
- Perform title search
- Check for liens, back taxes, and other encumbrances
- Inspect properties when possible
- Understand redemption rights
- Consult with real estate attorney

⚠️ **Sale Format**
- Sales are typically **public auctions**
- May require **cash or certified funds**
- May require **deposit or registration**
- Read sale terms carefully
