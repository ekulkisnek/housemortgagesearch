## 📋 LEGAL REQUIREMENTS FOR FORECLOSURE NOTICES

**Illinois Law Requires:**
- **Publication**: 3 times in newspaper
- **Timing**: First publication at least 21 days before sale
- **Content**: Property address, case number, sale date/time
- **Public Record**: All foreclosure notices are public records

**This Means:**
- ✅ Data is legally public
- ✅ You have right to access it
- ✅ Commercial services can legally aggregate it
- ✅ You can legally use subscription services
