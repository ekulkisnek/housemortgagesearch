# DuPage County Foreclosure Sales - Frequency, Timing & Access

## 📊 FREQUENCY OF FORECLOSURE SALES

### Regular Schedule
- **Sales Held**: Every Tuesday and Thursday at 10:00 AM
- **That's**: **2 days per week** = approximately **8-10 sales per month**
- **Location**: 501 N. County Farm Road, Wheaton, IL (Jail visitation lobby)

### Typical Volume
- **Per Sale Day**: Typically 5-20+ properties (varies)
- **Per Week**: Typically 10-40+ properties
- **Per Month**: Typically 40-160+ properties

**Note**: The number varies significantly based on:
- Economic conditions
- Time of year
- Court processing speed
- Lender activity

## ⏰ HOW EARLY ARE NEW LISTINGS POSTED?

### Advance Notice Requirements

**Legal Notice Requirements:**
- **Illinois Law**: Foreclosure sale notices must be published **3 times** before sale
- **First Publication**: Typically **3-4 weeks before** the sale date
- **Last Publication**: At least **7 days before** the sale date

**Typical Timeline:**
1. **Court enters judgment** → Sale scheduled ~3 months later
2. **4 weeks before sale**: Notice of sale published in newspapers
3. **3 weeks before sale**: Notice published again
4. **2 weeks before sale**: Notice published again
5. **1 week before sale**: Final notice, sale date confirmed
6. **Sale date**: Property auctioned

**So you can see listings:**
- **Earliest**: 3-4 weeks before sale
- **Most reliable**: 2-3 weeks before sale
- **Latest updates**: 1 week before sale

## 📍 WHERE TO SEE THE INFORMATION

### 1. DuPage County Sheriff's Office ⭐ **PRIMARY SOURCE**

**Methods:**
- **Phone**: (630) 407-2000 - Call for current listings
- **In Person**: Visit 501 N. County Farm Road, Wheaton, IL 60187
- **Best Time to Call**: Monday-Friday, 8 AM - 4:30 PM
- **Ask For**: "Current foreclosure sale listings" or "Schedule of upcoming sales"

**What They Provide:**
- Current week's sales
- Upcoming sales (next 2-4 weeks)
- Property addresses
- Case numbers
- Sale dates and times

**Limitation**: May not have a public online database - you need to call or visit
